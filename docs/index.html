Coordinator question What should I bring for Toyota LP?
Knowledge-based answer

Toyota LP is difficulty 1/5 and usually takes 30-60 minutes per unit. Bring longer display mounting screws, zip ties, cable protectors, spare connectors, a drill, Phillips bits, metric sockets, a multimeter, and a trim tool.

Field question Can Mia handle Jungheinrich EFG alone?
Knowledge-based answer

No. Jungheinrich EFG is difficulty 5/5 and requires relay preparation plus advanced diagnostics. Assign Priya Shah or another lead-level installer.

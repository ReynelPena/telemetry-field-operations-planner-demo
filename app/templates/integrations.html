Recommended End-to-End Flow

1HubSpot deal reaches PO received stage.
2Planner creates a job and validates mandatory installation data.
3Production updates carrier and tracking number when hardware is ready.
4Coordinator approves the AI route, technician, and schedule.

CRM PO Intake Endpoint

POST JSON to create a job when a HubSpot deal or PO requires field installation by your team.

POST /api/crm/hubspot/po 
{
  "source_system": "HubSpot",
  "hubspot_deal_id": "123456789",
  "po_number": "PO-12345",
  "customer_name": "Example Fleet",
  "customer_contact_email": "customer@example.com",
  "sales_rep_email": "sales@example.com",
  "installation_required": true,
  "requested_date": "2026-06-01",
  "site_city": "Dallas",
  "site_state": "TX",
  "vehicle_make": "Toyota",
  "vehicle_model": "LP",
  "fuel_type": "LP",
  "quantity": 10
}

Production Tracking Endpoint

POST when the production team has organized the order and shipping/tracking is available.

POST /api/production/tracking 
{
  "po_number": "PO-12345",
  "shipment_carrier": "UPS",
  "shipment_tracking_number": "1Z999999999",
  "order_ready": true
}

Confirmation Rule

A job may exist as soon as the PO arrives. However, it will remain in Waiting Required Info or Pending Approval until the required fields, production tracking, AI recommendation, and coordinator approval are complete.
